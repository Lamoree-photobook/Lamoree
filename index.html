The Story

Every frame holds a breath

Lámoree began as a personal project — a way to slow down and truly hold the moments that matter. Not just print them, but feel them.

Unlike off-the-shelf photobooks, every Lámoree book starts with you. We hand you the template — the layout, the structure, the creative foundation and you bring it to life with your own photos, words and memories. The result is something no one else in the world has.

Questions

FAQs

How long does shipping take?

Once your photobook design has been confirmed, your order will be carefully printed, bound and delivered within approximately 14 working days across Singapore.

How do I pay?

Payment is via Paynow, Bank Transfer or Paylah. Details will be shared with you on WhatsApp / Telegram after you submit your order.

Can I order outside Singapore?

Currently, physical photobooks are available for delivery within Singapore only. For international customers, our digital photobook templates remain available so you can create and preserve your memories wherever you are.

Can I change my design after confirmation?

Once your design has been confirmed, your photobook will proceed to production and changes can no longer be made. Please review your design carefully before final approval.

Can I request a refund after placing an order?

As each photobook is custom-made and personalized, we are unable to offer refunds once payment has been made and production has begun. If your order arrives damaged or there is a printing issue, please contact us within 48 hours of receiving your order with photos, and we will work with you to resolve the issue.
