The Story

Every frame holds a breath

This photobook began as a personal project — a way to slow down and truly see the places I was passing through. Each image was chosen not for its drama, but for the feeling it holds.

Printed on archival matte paper with an open-flat binding, the book is designed to be held, read slowly, and returned to.
